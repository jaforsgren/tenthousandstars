Final status: {PlayerFaction} has achieved decisive victory over {EnemyFaction} within {SectorName}. Enemy command structure dismantled. Remaining opposition eliminated, scattered, or withdrawn beyond operational reach.

The war is over.

The records will agree.

You still don’t.

**VISUAL CALCULUS [Medium: Success]**
The map is still lit.

That’s what your mind keeps refusing.

Systems under control. Supply lines reconnected. Defensive grids reactivated. Everything in its proper place again.

But you see what it cost to get it there.

Empty corridors. Burned trade lanes. Stations that will never fully function again, even if they say they will.

The sector is stable.

It looks wrong.

**LOGIC [Easy: Success]**
You won.

By every measurable metric.

{EnemyFaction} can no longer sustain operations in {SectorName}. No coordinated resistance remains. Strategic initiative fully removed.

This is what success looks like.

You expected it to feel different.

It doesn’t.

**HALF LIGHT [Medium: Success]**
There should be relief.

There isn’t.

There is only the absence of incoming reports. The silence where alarms used to be. The reflex to check channels that no longer need checking.

Your body still waits for the next failure.

It doesn’t come.

That feels like another kind of threat.

**EMPATHY [Hard: Success]**
You read the casualty summaries.

Once.

Then again.

Then you stop reading them linearly and start scanning for anomalies—numbers that feel wrong, places where names blur into counts.

There are too many.

You remember some of them.

You wish you didn’t.

You stop telling yourself it was necessary.

That part of you stopped working somewhere mid-war.

**AUTHORITY [Medium: Success]**
{MissionObjective}: achieved.

You say it out loud once.

It sounds like someone else’s voice using your mouth.

There is nothing left to issue orders toward. Only stabilization. Reconstruction. Occupation of what is already taken.

The word “victory” appears in reports.

You don’t correct it.

You can’t afford to.

**VOLITION [Hard: Success]**
You are still functional.

That is not the same as intact.

You continue because stopping would require meeting what this has done to you. There is no structure left to fall back on that is not built out of what you ordered.

So you keep ordering.

Smaller things now. Safer things.

As if that changes anything.

**ENCYCLOPEDIA [Trivial: Success]**

Victory recorded.

Irreversibly.

**INLAND EMPIRE [Hard: Success]**
The sector is quiet.

Not peaceful.

Quiet in the way a place is quiet after something has been removed from it that cannot be put back.

You look at it and you recognize it, but only in pieces. Systems, routes, names. The structure is familiar. The meaning is not.

There are survivors.

There are rebuild orders.

There are protocols for recovery.

You understand all of them.

You just don’t feel like the person who should be allowed to approve them.

Because somewhere between the first engagement and this silence, something in you stopped returning intact.

The war is over.

And your mind is still fighting it.
